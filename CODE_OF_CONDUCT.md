# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

This project aims to maintain an open, respectful, and collaborative community.

## Expected behavior

- technical and respectful communication;
- criticism of ideas, not people;
- inclusive collaboration;
- responsibility for the impact of one's words and actions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, exposure of personal data, personal attacks, and any conduct that harms collaboration.

## Enforcement

Reports should be directed to the project maintainers through the repository's
[GitHub contact page](https://github.com/evandroluizvieira/EToolkit/issues). Do not include sensitive
personal details in a public issue; maintainers will provide a private channel when necessary.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.
